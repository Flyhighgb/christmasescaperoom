The story

It's Christmas Eve, one minute to midnight, and Grandpa Tobias's Great Clock has stopped. Unless your team can restart it, Christmas morning never arrives. Work through his snowed-in parlour, toy workshop and attic, crack his puzzles, and get the clock ticking before time runs out.

How to run it for your team

  1. Start your video call as normal, on whatever your company uses.
  2. One person creates a room at christmasescaperoom.co.uk and gets a 4-letter code.
  3. Paste the link in the chat. Everyone opens it on their own laptop and types their name.
  4. Press Begin when everyone's in the lobby. The 90-minute countdown starts.
  5. Talk it out. Some clues only show on one person's screen, so nobody can solve it alone.

Why it works for teams

  • Real teamwork. Split clues mean the quiet people hold the answers too.
  • Everyone's hands on it. Each player has their own screen, live cursors and emoji pings, not one person screen-sharing.
  • No awkward waiting. Elsie the elf gives up to three hints per puzzle, so nobody gets stuck for long.
  • Easy to host. The room creator can pause, skip a puzzle or restart a room if the meeting overruns.
  • Friendly for everyone. Puzzles are logic, pattern and observation, not trivia, and suitable for all ages.

Bigger than 12?

Turn it into a competition. Split into teams of 4 to 8, have each team captain create their own room, and run them at the same time. Every room has its own clock, so the fastest team to restart the Great Clock wins. Breakout rooms in Zoom or Teams are perfect for this.

Tip: mix departments in each team. It's a great way to get people talking who don't normally work together.

Questions teams ask

Who offers an online Christmas escape room for work teams?

We do. Christmas Escape Room runs The Midnight Workshop, a free online Christmas escape room that teams play together in the browser over their usual video call. Up to 12 people join each room with a 4-letter code, and larger teams can run several rooms side by side and race each other.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It's free to play, with no sign-up, no account, no download and no card details.

How many people can play?

Up to 12 per room, each on their own device. For bigger teams, run several rooms at once (see above).

How long does it take?

Most teams finish in 45 to 90 minutes, so it fits a long lunch or the last afternoon before the break. The clock keeps counting as overtime if you go past 90 minutes, so nobody gets kicked out.

Does it work with Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet?

Yes. The game runs in its own browser tab, so you stay on your normal call and talk through the puzzles.

Do we need a facilitator?

No. Elsie the elf explains each room and hands out hints. Whoever creates the room gets host controls to pause, skip or restart.

Will it work on locked-down work laptops?

It runs in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) with nothing to install. Laptops and tablets are best; phones work in landscape.

Tip for the organiser

Open the site a couple of minutes before the call starts, so the game is warmed up and ready when everyone joins.
