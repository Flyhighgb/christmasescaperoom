# Solutions (spoilers!)

## Room 1: The Parlour
1. **The locked present.** The gift tag shows red, gold, blue, purple dots (with 2+ players, each person sees only some of them). Count baubles on the tree: red 4, gold 2, blue 5, purple 3. Code **4253**. Reward: red glass lens.
2. **The Christmas cards.** Click the cards on the mantelpiece. It's a pairs memory game: match all 8 pairs. Reward: brass door key.
3. **The workshop door.** Open Grandpa's letter on the desk and move the red lens over the red scribbles. Hidden word: **CINNAMON**. The door needs the door key as well as the word.

## Room 2: Grandpa's Workshop
4. **The bell melody.** The torn melody is split between players (see "Your pocket"). Pieces in snowflake order: ❄ blue green, ❄❄ silver blue, ❄❄❄ green gold, ❄❄❄❄ red gold. Ring: **blue, green, silver, blue, green, gold, red, gold**. A wrong tune resets. Reward: brass gear.
5. **The scrambled painting.** Rotate all 9 tiles upright. No item, but two clues: the banner reads **"★ under F"** and the village clock shows **7:30**.
6. **The photo album.** On the workbench. Spot 5 differences: snowman's nose, star on the tree, a lit window, sledge colour, the moon. Reward: silver gear.
7. **The toy cabinet.** Ledger logic puzzle (clues are shared out between players): Pip = kite, Bramble = drum, Tansy = rocking horse, Juniper = teddy bear. Set dials, pull the handle. Reward: star key.
8. **The hatch.** Click the ceiling hatch above the ladder to use the star key. The attic opens.

## Room 3: The Clock Tower
9. **The pocket watch.** On the side table. Set it to **7:30** (the time from the painting) and open it. Reward: copper gear.
10. **The clock mechanism.** Click the three empty spindles to fit the brass, silver and copper gears.
11. **The engraved plaque.** Frost hides different letters from each player; the full code is OJBZOCZM. Turn the cipher wheel so the star (inner A) is under F. Each engraved letter on the inner ring sits under its real letter: OJBZOCZM → **TOGETHER**. Type it into the plaque.

## Kid job: the hidden mice (optional)
Three mice hide in every room. Finding all three makes Elsie tell a secret clue.
- Parlour: on the curtain rail, by the fireplace on the floor, on top of the workshop door. Clue: the spice starts with C.
- Workshop: on the toy shelf by the ball, on the window sill, on the workbench crossbar. Clue: Tansy made the horse, Pip the kite.
- Clock Tower: on the roof beam, on the round window sill, under the side table. Clue: O means T, Z means E.
