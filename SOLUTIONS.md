# Solutions (spoilers!)

## Room 1: The Parlour
1. **The locked present.** The gift tag shows red, gold, blue, purple dots. Count baubles on the tree: red 4, gold 2, blue 5, purple 3. Code **4253**. Reward: red glass lens.
2. **The workshop door.** Open Grandpa's letter on the desk and move the red lens over the red scribbles. Hidden word: **CINNAMON**. Type it into the door lock.

## Room 2: Grandpa's Workshop
3. **The bell melody.** The torn melody is split between players (see "Pocket"). Pieces in snowflake order: ❄ blue green, ❄❄ silver blue, ❄❄❄ green gold, ❄❄❄❄ red gold. Ring: **blue, green, silver, blue, green, gold, red, gold**. Reward: brass gear.
4. **The scrambled painting.** Rotate all 9 tiles upright. Reward: silver gear, and the banner reads **"★ under F"**.
5. **The toy cabinet.** Ledger logic puzzle: Pip = kite, Bramble = drum, Tansy = rocking horse, Juniper = teddy bear. Set dials, pull the handle. Reward: star key.
6. **The hatch.** Click the ceiling hatch above the ladder to use the star key. The attic opens.

## Room 3: The Clock Tower
7. **The clock mechanism.** Click the two empty spindles to fit the brass and silver gears.
8. **The engraved plaque.** Turn the cipher wheel so the star (inner A) is under F. Each engraved letter on the inner ring sits under its real letter: OJBZOCZM → **TOGETHER**. Type it into the plaque.
